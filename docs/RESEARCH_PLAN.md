# User research plan (no interviews have been run; everything below is to be executed)

## Recruits (first round)
8 buyers (New Cairo/West Cairo off-plan, mixed budget). 3 renters. 2 people buying for relatives from abroad (H-05). 3 developer sales staff. 2 brokers. 1 field sales person. Incentive and consent forms: founder approval needed.

## Arabic interview guide (buyers), 45 min
1. احكيلي عن آخر مرة شفت إعلان شقة عجبك. وصلك إزاي؟ (واتساب، إعلان، سمسار؟) ممكن توريني الرسالة؟
2. إيه أول حاجة عملتها بعد ما شفت السعر؟
3. إيه المعلومات اللي كنت محتاجها قبل ما تروح تعاين، ومالقيتهاش؟
4. حصل قبل كده إن السعر أو المتاح اختلف لما رحت؟ إيه اللي حصل؟
5. مين بيشاركك القرار؟ بتبعتوا لبعض إيه؟
6. لما بتقارن بين عرضين، بتقارن بإيه؟ (المقدم؟ القسط؟ الإجمالي؟ الاستلام؟)
7. (H-03) لو قدامك رقم واحد بس عن الشقة، تختار أنهي: السعر الإجمالي، المقدم، المطلوب في أول سنة، ولا القسط؟ ليه؟
8. إزاي بتعرف إن العرض لسه ساري؟ بتثق في مين؟
9. (H-02) لو شفت «قائمة أسعار المطوّر، ٣ نوفمبر» مقابل علامة «موثّق»، تثق في أنهي أكتر؟
10. البوابة اللي بتدخل منها، والمواصلات: بتعرفهم إزاي قبل الزيارة؟
11. بعد المعاينة، إيه اللي بتكتبه أو بتفتكره؟

## Usability tasks (pass/fail; Arabic-first on a mid-range phone)
| # | Task | Pass if |
|---|---|---|
| U-1 | "Find a 3-bedroom apartment in New Cairo you could start with under EGP 1 million." | Reaches a matching result card without help in ≤ 3 minutes |
| U-2 | "For this unit, what's due before the end of 2027, and who said so on what date?" | States the amount and the source line correctly |
| U-3 | "Compare these two; which needs more cash in the first year? Why can't you say which is cheaper overall?" | Mentions the unknown parking fee |
| U-4 | "Share this comparison with your sister, then stop her seeing it." | Link shared, then revoked |
| U-5 | "Ask to visit Friday at 10. What happens next?" | Says it is not confirmed until the seller replies |
| U-6 | "How would you get to the visitor gate from Point 90 Mall on foot?" | Reads the route and notices "not verified on site" |

## Supplier walkthrough
Hand over the template. Observe them filling it from their real sheet (their device, their file never leaves their control during the session). Measure time to the first valid upload, the error types, and which fields they don't have.

## Like-for-like benchmark (brief §3)
Five tasks across two existing services and Wadeh, on the same dated search:
1. Locate an identifiable available unit.
2. Find all mandatory cash due before handover.
3. Verify the last price change.
4. Route to a visitor entrance.
5. Learn who receives a viewing request.

Record completion, taps or time, source quality and the first point of failure. Keep the results private if a site's terms restrict republication. **Not yet run:** it needs a person, a phone and lawful access. Prediction to falsify: Wadeh wins task 2 (cash due before handover) and loses task 1 (inventory breadth).
